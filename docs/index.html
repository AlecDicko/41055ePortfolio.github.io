About Me

I am a final-semester mechatronic engineering student at UTS who works most comfortably at the seam between disciplines, where a sensor signal must survive its way through analogue conditioning, into firmware, and out the other side as something a machine can act on.

What drives that is specific: I want my work to make people safer. Whether that's defence systems looking out for soldiers in the field, or factory automation that pulls workers out of harm's way, I'm interested in smart systems that carry real risk away from real people.

University has pushed that interest into actual capability. I'm confident across SOLIDWORKS and KiCad, comfortable with electrical systems, PCBs, mechanical assemblies and code, and I've built the communication skills to match through group work, presentations and a stint as a casual tutor, where I guide other students toward understanding rather than doing the work for them.

Outside the workshop, I'm usually under the bonnet of my own car, snowboarding, or camping in the great outdoors. I like being hands-on as much off the clock as on it!
